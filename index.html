Free apps that run on your Mac.

Harbor installs small, private apps with one click. They run on your computer, work offline, and keep your data on your Mac.

Get Harbor

For macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon

Opening Harbor the first time
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Harbor into Applications.
  2. Open Harbor. macOS says it can't check it for malicious software, because Harbor is free and not registered with Apple. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Harbor. Confirm with your password.

You only do this once. Harbor updates itself after that.

  • Runs on your MacNo accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions.
  • Your data stays hereEverything you make is saved on this computer.
  • One click to installAnd one click to remove.
