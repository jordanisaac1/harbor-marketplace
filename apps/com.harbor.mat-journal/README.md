# Mat Journal

A training journal for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu that turns every class into progress you can see.

## After every class, a quick check-in

Tell Mat Journal when you usually train and it asks how it went once class is over. The check-in takes about a minute:

- What kind of session it was and how long you trained
- How hard it was, your energy, and how you feel about it
- The techniques you worked on, and which ones you pulled off in sparring
- Rounds rolled, submissions you landed, and times you tapped
- What went well, what to work on, and any soreness

## Level up

Every session earns experience points. Climb from Mat Rookie to Living Legend, keep a weekly streak alive, take on three new objectives every week, and unlock 29 achievements along the way, from your first tap to your hundredth hour on the mats.

## Track every technique

More than 80 techniques come built in, from takedowns and guard to leg locks, and you can add your own. Move each one from Learning to Drilling to Applying in rolls, Reliable and Mastered. Mat Journal suggests when one is ready to level up, and keeps notes and a history for each.

## Your goals, your way

Set the goals that matter to you: train three times a week, spend 20 hours on the mats this month, hit ten triangles in live rolls, make your knee slice reliable, or anything else you want to track yourself. Progress fills in automatically from your check-ins.

## Also inside

- A calendar of every class you've trained, planned or skipped
- Your belt, stripes and promotion history
- Charts of your mat time, submissions, and game by position
- Download your sessions as a spreadsheet, or a full backup, any time

Everything stays on your Mac.
